## Step 2 — Project Overview

### Project Title
**Hippocrates AI**

### One-Line Summary
An AI-powered learning ecosystem that personalizes student learning, simplifies educators' supervision, and empowers parental oversight.

### Project Description *(minimum 50 characters)*

Hippocrates AI is an educational technology platform that applies mastery-based learning principles — specifically Spaced Repetition and interleaved practice — to help students learn faster, more deeply, and more enjoyably. Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots that answer questions on demand, Hippocrates AI is a structured, domain-specific system: students upload their lecture materials, and the platform uses Google Gemini to generate contextual quizzes and flashcards referenced directly against that content. Students then work through a personalized spaced repetition queue that requires them to answer each question correctly three consecutive times before the system considers it mastered and schedules it for future review. The platform tracks daily streaks, mastery counts, and progress across materials, and is designed to eventually expose these metrics to educators and parents in real time — replacing the current reality where parents wait until the end of a school term to learn how their child is doing.

## Step 3 — Venture Details

### Who experiences this problem?

**Students:**
Secondary and post-secondary students face passive learning environments and cognitive overload from dense curricula. There is no structured feedback loop telling them what they actually know versus what they merely read once.

**Educators:**
Educators lack tools to deliver personalized, 1-to-1 feedback at scale. A teacher with 40 students cannot realistically track each student's individual comprehension gaps.

**Parents:**
Parents who invest in their child's education have no visibility into daily academic progress. They rely on expensive private tutors or remain uninformed until end-of-term report cards arrive.

---

### How does that group currently handle the problem without this venture?

**Students:**
Students re-read notes passively, rely on last-minute cramming, or use general-purpose AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini) for explanations — with no structured retention system and no guarantee that knowledge is being retained.

**Educators:**
Educators rely on periodic examinations and manual observation to gauge understanding. Between tests, there is no real-time visibility into which students are struggling and on what topics.

**Parents:**
Parents pay for private tutoring (which is expensive and scales poorly) or wait for school report cards — receiving feedback weeks or months after a problem has already developed.

---

### What has the venture built so far, and what does it currently do?

Hippocrates AI is a working web and mobile application. Students can upload lecture materials (PDFs, DOCX files) to their personal library. The AI (powered by Google Gemini) reads their material and, from any section a student highlights, generates four types of assessment questions: multiple choice, true/false clusters, fill-in-the-gap, and select-all-that-apply. Students review pending questions before approving them into their personalised spaced repetition queue. The queue uses a custom algorithm — requiring 3 consecutive correct answers to master a card — and reschedules mastered cards at 3-to-5-day intervals for long-term retention. Daily progress streaks and mastery counts are tracked on a personal dashboard.

---

### What has not been built yet?

**Calculation-based learning modules:** The current system is optimised for factual and conceptual recall. Quantitative subjects — engineering mathematics, physics problem-solving, chemistry equations — require a different interaction model that has not yet been built.

**Broad database and keyword-based auto-generation:** The current system generates questions from student-uploaded materials. A curated question database covering standard secondary and university curricula, searchable by keyword or topic, is planned but not yet implemented.

**Parent and educator dashboard:** Third-party access — allowing parents and educators to view a student's real-time progress metrics — is planned as a near-term priority but has not been built yet.

---

### What does the venture charge, and per what unit?

**Currently:** A credit-based pricing model. Students and parents purchase AI credits to power question generation and session reviews. Pricing is set at ₦10,000 for 1,000 credits, displayed transparently on the landing page. Credits are consumed per AI generation request.

**Long-term:** A B2B institutional model where schools, departments, and eventually government education bodies pay a subscription fee covering all students within that institution — eliminating the per-student payment friction entirely.

---

### Why is this team able to build this?

The team built Hippocrates AI because we personally lived the problem. As students navigating bulky medical and technical curricula, we experienced first-hand the inadequacy of passive reading, last-minute cramming, and the absence of structured feedback. We recognised that the problem was not unique to us — it is systemic across Nigerian and African secondary and tertiary education. We also identified that AI, specifically large language models grounded in educational context, could solve it in a way that was previously impossible. That lived urgency, combined with hands-on technical capability in full-stack development and AI integration, is why we are uniquely positioned to build and iterate on this product.

---

### Who pays for this?

**Currently:** Individual students and parents purchase AI credits directly via the B2C model. The transaction is simple: a parent or student buys a credit bundle on the platform and uses it to power their learning sessions.

**Long-term:** As the product scales to institutional deployments — covering entire departments or schools — the paying entity transitions to the institution or government ministry of education, enabling a B2B revenue model with larger contract values and predictable recurring revenue.

---

### Name the closest existing product and state the one thing this venture does differently.

**Closest product:** Mindgrasp AI (US-based) — an AI platform that reads uploaded documents and generates study tools from them.

**Key differentiator:** Hippocrates AI is built specifically for the African educational context, with a primary differentiator of **third-party parental and educator access**. In African households, parents are active stakeholders in their children's education — they are often the ones paying. Mindgrasp AI has no mechanism for a parent to log in and see their child's progress in real time. Hippocrates AI is designed from the ground up to make that visibility possible, turning the parent from a passive payer into an informed participant in their child's academic journey.

## Step 6 — Use of Funds

### If funded, what specifically becomes possible within 8 months that is not possible without the grant?

Without this grant, Hippocrates AI remains constrained to a small pool of self-funded users. AI generation is the core of the product, and API costs currently limit how many students can actively use the platform before credits run out. The grant unlocks three things that are presently impossible:

**1. Scale without credit rationing.** With AI credit provisioning fully funded, we can onboard hundreds of students without throttling their access to question generation — the core value of the platform.

**2. Handle institutional load.** Our current server infrastructure handles small user counts. Scaling to a 200-student department — the entry point for any B2B contract — requires the AWS infrastructure upgrade this grant makes possible.

**3. Build the parent dashboard.** The feature that most differentiates us from US competitors — real-time parental visibility into student progress — cannot be built without engineering resources this grant funds. Completing it opens the B2B sales conversation with secondary schools, because it directly addresses what institutions need to justify the purchase: accountability for outcomes.

Together, these three milestones transform Hippocrates AI from a working personal tool into a commercially deployable institutional product within 8 months.
