lightbulb Clinical Explanation & High-Yield Pearl

Troponin I elevates at 3–4 hours, peaks around 24 hours, and remains elevated for 7–10 days, providing superior diagnostic sensitivity and cardiac specificity over CK-MB and LDH. While myoglobin elevates sooner (1–3 hours), it lacks myocardial specificity and is rapidly cleared by renal filtration.
